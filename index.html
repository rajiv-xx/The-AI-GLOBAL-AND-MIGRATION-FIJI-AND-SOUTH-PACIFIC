COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide IELTS or PTE preparation support?

Yes. AI Global currently lists IELTS and PTE preparation support, including practice and guidance designed around a student's target score.

What services do you provide?

Services include education counselling, student accommodation support, skills assessment information, scholarships, health insurance assistance, career counselling, document assistance and student visa services.

Can I get help choosing a course?

Yes. We can discuss your interests, previous studies and future goals before you choose a course or institution.

Which countries can AI Global assist with?

The main AI Global website states that its network supports applications across 20+ countries, including Australia, New Zealand, USA, UK, Canada, Germany, Ireland and Singapore.

How long does a visa application take?

Processing time varies by country, visa type and individual circumstances. Current processing guidance should be checked before applying.

How can I book a consultation?

Contact the Fiji and South Pacific team by phone, email or through the contact details below to discuss your requirements.
