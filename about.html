Fiji & South Pacific

OUR APPROACH

Support from course selection to application preparation

Course and institution selection

The main AI Global service model includes counselling to help students identify courses and institutions that fit their goals.

  • Discuss previous study and career interests
  • Compare suitable course and institution options
  • Assist with application and offer-letter processes

Application preparation

AI Global lists document assistance covering items such as statements of purpose, letters of recommendation and visa paperwork.

  • Organise required documents
  • Review application information
  • Support communication with education providers

Scholarship and financial guidance

The organisation also promotes scholarship assistance for international students.

  • Identify possible scholarship opportunities
  • Check basic eligibility information
  • Prepare supporting material where appropriate

Career and skills planning

Career counselling and skills assessment are included in the main service offering.

  • Discuss career direction
  • Understand skills assessment pathways
  • Consider study choices alongside future goals
Important: The Fiji website is a local service site associated with AI Global Education & Migration. Course availability, visa rules, fees and eligibility can change, so current requirements should always be confirmed before an application is submitted.
